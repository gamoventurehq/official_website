## Scope notes and terms

- **Timing and allowances.** Production starts after Gamoventure receives the advance payment, complete brief, required content and assets, and necessary account access. Waiting time for client feedback is excluded. Page and section counts are maximum allowances, not targets to fill.
- **Feedback and changes.** Each revision round is one consolidated feedback list from one client decision-maker. A new design direction, additional pages, or new functionality changes the scope. Fixes to Gamoventure's own implementation defects do not consume a revision round.
- **Client-controlled services.** Domain registration, web hosting, business email, paid assets, paid plugins, and other third-party services are funded by the client and remain in client-controlled accounts.
- **Post-launch defect window.** A 30-day post-launch defect-reporting window covers reproducible failures within the agreed delivered scope, excluding enhancements, client-made edits, or third-party outages. Ongoing maintenance and content updates are quoted separately.
- **Search expectations.** Technical search foundations and analytics setup do not guarantee search rankings, traffic, enquiries, or sales.

## Excluded from both fixed website packages

- CMS or CRM functionality
- Ecommerce, payment collection, or online ordering
- Booking systems, user accounts, or private dashboards
- File uploads, automated quotations, or custom integrations
- Multilingual content or translation
- Extensive research or legal-policy drafting
- Logo design, full brand identity, photography, 3D work, or promotional video
- Ongoing hosting, maintenance, SEO, or marketing services
